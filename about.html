Who We Are

More Than a Network — We're a Movement

Sazinaldo Dust Roots Consultancy was founded in 2026 in Ekurhuleni, Gauteng, with a singular conviction: that South African women's football is rich with world-class talent that simply lacks the right pathway to global opportunity.

We bridge that gap. As a privately held specialist consultancy of 2–10 dedicated professionals, we combine deep local scouting intelligence with a verified global agent and league network spanning over 40 leagues across six continents.

Our name carries meaning — "dust roots" speaks to the raw, gritty beginnings where South Africa's finest talent is forged, often on uneven pitches with minimal resources, yet with an abundance of heart, skill, and determination. We exist to honour that origin while unlocking its global destiny.

Our Mission

To create a sustainable ecosystem where regional South African female football excellence meets professional global opportunity — by identifying, protecting, and elevating players through ethical, transparent, and verified pathways to the world's top leagues.

🔭

Our Vision

To see South African players leading in the top divisions of world football, while fostering a self-sustaining, professional ecosystem for our local clubs — one that generates revenue, builds pride, and inspires the next generation of female footballers across the continent.

The Movement

The 'Fight to Play' Ethos

As Global Ambassador Lead for the "Fight to Play" movement, Sazinaldo stands at the intersection of sport and social justice — championing gender equality in football worldwide.

🌍

UN SDG 5 Alignment

Our Shared Value Strategy is anchored in the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 5 — Gender Equality — ensuring women's football receives equal investment and opportunity.

📺

Broadcast & Media Strategy

We drive international broadcast production strategy to increase visibility for women's football, bringing SA talent to global audiences and attracting commercial partnerships.

🏗️

Infrastructure Development

Advocating for and facilitating grassroots infrastructure development — better pitches, facilities, and coaching environments for female players at every level.

📜

Legacy & Documentary

Producing legacy content and documentary work that tells the stories of South Africa's female footballers, preserving history and inspiring future generations.
