Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit myself or a player for scouting consideration? +

Use the contact form above, select "Player / Athlete" as your role and "Player Placement & Scouting" as the subject. Include as much detail as possible — age, position, current club, stats, and any footage links. We review all submissions and respond within 48 hours.

How do I know if an agent or scout is legitimate? +

Legitimate FIFA-licensed agents are registered with FIFA and their national federation. They will never ask you to pay upfront fees. If you're unsure about an approach you've received, contact us immediately — we offer free verification checks for South African players.

What does club professionalisation involve? +

We help clubs register as NPOs, integrate with the MY SAFA platform for official status, establish governance structures, set up transfer and sell-on fee agreements, and access coaching certification pathways. This creates sustainable revenue streams and formal competition eligibility.

Do you work with players of all ages? +

We work with senior players (18+) for direct league placement. For younger players (under 18), we focus on educational grant pathways, development planning, and positioning for future placement. All work involving minors follows strict ethical and legal guidelines.

How can brands and sponsors get involved? +

We welcome conversations with brands whose values align with women's football, gender equality, and youth development. Contact us with "Commercial Sponsorship" as your subject and we'll schedule a discovery call to explore partnership opportunities tailored to your goals.
