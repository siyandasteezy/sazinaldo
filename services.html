Global Talent Bridge

Our flagship service connects elite South African female players with verified scouts, licensed agents, and club recruitment officers in the world's top-tier leagues. Every connection is vetted, transparent, and structured to protect the player's interests above all else.

We don't just open doors — we walk players through them, providing guidance on contracts, relocation, cultural adaptation, and professional conduct at each stage of the placement process.

  • Personalised player profiles and highlight reels distributed to our verified league network
  • Direct introductions to licensed agents with active mandates in target leagues
  • Trial facilitation and club visit coordination
  • Contract review guidance and negotiation support
  • Post-placement welfare check-ins and ongoing advisory

    Player Protection & Ethical Matchmaking

    The women's football transfer market is unfortunately rife with predatory actors — fake agents, fraudulent trials, and exploitative contracts that damage careers and lives. Sazinaldo exists to change that reality for South African players.

    We operate a strict verification system for all agents and scouts within our network, and we educate players on their rights, red flags, and the proper pathways to international football.

  • Agent licence verification through FIFA and national federation registries
  • "Hoax Agent" awareness workshops for players and families
  • Contract red-flag screening before any player commitment
  • SAFA and FIFA regulatory guidance on international transfers
  • Safe trial facilitation with club-level due diligence
  • Player rights education and ongoing welfare monitoring
    • 🏟️

    Club Professionalization

    For South African women's football to truly flourish, local clubs need the administrative and legal infrastructure to operate as sustainable, professional entities — not just volunteer-run passion projects. We help transform that.

    Through our club professionalisation service, we guide regional clubs through the processes that unlock funding, formal competition entry, and the ability to earn from their players' success when they move abroad.

  • NPO (Non-Profit Organisation) registration guidance and support
  • MY SAFA platform integration for official club status
  • Sell-on fee structures and transfer agreement templates
  • Club governance frameworks and constitution drafting
  • Coach and administrator certification pathways
  • High-performance training hub development consulting
  • Sovereign Sports and SAFA alignment advisory
    • 📡

    Visibility & Commercial Sponsorship

    Talent without visibility is opportunity lost. We build professional digital footprints for players and clubs, lobby governing bodies for expanded competition structures, and connect commercial sponsors with the compelling stories of South African women's football.

  • Player digital profile creation — highlight reels, stats packages, and professional bios
  • Social media strategy and personal brand development for athletes
  • Lobbying for expanded Sasol League and SAFA women's competition structures
  • Commercial sponsorship strategy — matching brands to clubs and athletes
  • International broadcast production strategy for SA women's football coverage
  • Legacy content and documentary production
  • Media relations and press coverage coordination
    • 🎓

    Athlete Educational & Athletic Grants

    A player's career is finite. We believe in the whole athlete — ensuring that South African female footballers have access to educational opportunities alongside their sporting careers, creating pathways to life beyond the pitch as well as on it.

  • Educational grant identification and application support
  • Athletic scholarship facilitation — US colleges and international programs
  • Academic and sporting dual-pathway planning for young players
  • Partnerships with educational institutions that value elite athletes
  • Mentor matching with former professional players and academics
    • 💼
